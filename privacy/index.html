Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Civics Mentor is a free study app for the U.S. citizenship civics test, made by an independent developer. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: the app has no accounts, keeps your progress on your phone, and does not sell your data. Google AdMob (for ads) and Sentry (for crash reports) receive some data, as described below.

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to USCIS or any government agency. Questions and answers come from the official USCIS civics test materials at https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship.

Data that stays on your phone

The app saves these items only on your phone:

  • Your settings: test version, 65/20 choice, state or territory, representative, voice and reminder choices, and interview date.
  • Your study history: your answers, scores, streak, and mastery of each question.
  • Notes you create with "I said it right" in voice answers: the question and the text of what you said.

We do not receive this data. You can save a copy as a file, load it on another phone, or delete it with "Reset progress" in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Microphone and speech

The app uses the microphone only after you tap the mic button to answer by voice. Your phone's speech recognition service turns your speech into text. When your phone supports it, this happens on the phone. Otherwise your phone's speech service, for example Google's on Android, may process the audio on its servers under its own privacy policy. The app does not record, store, or send your voice. It checks the text against the official answers on your phone.

Read aloud uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech and stays on your phone.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules it on your phone. No server is involved.

Answer updates

When you open the app, it downloads a small file of officeholder answers from this site, which GitHub Pages hosts. The request sends no personal data from the app. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and may log it. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free because it shows a small number of ads from Google AdMob: banner ads on some screens and optional video ads you choose to watch. There are no ads during a mock interview or on question screens. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location based on the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice in Settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Crash reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Sentry so the problem can be fixed. A report contains the error, the screen where it happened, the app version, and your phone's model and operating system version. It does not contain your name, email, answers, or voice. See the Sentry Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics beyond crash reports.
  • No location permission. Your state is what you choose in the app.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information for money.

Children

The app is made for adults preparing for the naturalization interview. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete your study data with "Reset progress" in Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Turn voice answers and reminders on or off in Settings.
  • Change your ad consent choice in Settings, and manage your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
