Civics Mentor

A free study app for the civics part of the U.S. naturalization interview. Practice the official 2025 and 2008 civics questions, learn why each answer is right, and take mock interviews that follow the official rules.

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to USCIS or any government agency. Questions and answers come from the official USCIS civics test materials at https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship.

What you get

  • Every official question and every answer USCIS accepts, word for word.
  • A short explanation and a memory tip for each answer, plus 45 plain-English articles.
  • Mock interviews, 65/20 mode, flashcards, and review of your mistakes.
  • Read aloud and answer by voice.
  • Answers for your state: senators, representative, governor, and capital.
  • Works offline. No account. Your progress stays on your phone.

Need legal help?

This app cannot give advice about your own immigration case. USCIS explains who can legally help you and how to avoid scams: uscis.gov: Find legal services.

Officeholder answers

Some answers change with elections, such as the names of the President or your senators. The app checks this site for updated answers, so you get them without an app update. The file is checked every week against official sources. Always give the name of the person serving on the day of your interview. USCIS lists changes at uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates.
